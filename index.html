About

I am a Research Scientist at Salesforce AI Research in Palo Alto. My current research focuses on self-improving language models and agents: how they can learn from experience, develop reusable memory and reason more effectively. I also work on retrieval and embedding models for text, code and multimodal data, as well as code intelligence.

I was a lead contributor to the SFR-Embedding models, which ranked first on the MTEB leaderboard, overall and on retrieval, at release in 2024, and led CodeXEmbed, a code embedding family reported to rank first on the CoIR benchmark. Many of these models are openly released and have been downloaded millions of times on Hugging Face. Before Salesforce, I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Illinois Chicago, where I worked on question answering, text generation and graph mining.

Research themes

Four threads that run through my work, each with a few representative papers.

Reasoning & self-improving agents

Making LLMs reason more reliably through verification, self-refinement and memory distilled from their own experience, and measuring logical reasoning with human-written benchmarks.

  • Procedural Memory Distillation NeurIPS 2026
  • Collaborative Verification arXiv 2024

Retrieval & embedding models

General-purpose embedding and reranking models for text, code, images and video, from training recipes and hard-batch mining to openly released models.

  • SFR-Embedding-2 2024
  • CodeXEmbed COLM 2025
  • VLM2Vec-V2 TMLR 2026

Code intelligence

Ranking and retrieval for software engineering, such as localizing issues in real repositories, and evaluating how well LLMs generate and judge code.

  • SweRank ICLR 2026
  • SweRank+ arXiv 2025
  • L2CEval TACL 2024
  • LLMs as code-summary evaluators IEEE TSE 2025

Question answering, parsing & generation

Earlier work on open-domain and knowledge-base QA, unified semantic parsing, knowledge-grounded text generation and graph mining.

  • Dense Hierarchical Retrieval Findings of EMNLP 2021
